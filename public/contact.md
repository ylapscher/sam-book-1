# Contact Sam Story Book

Use this page to reach the human who makes **Our Family Story Book**. Sam Story Book is a small studio, not a call center. For orders, start with the personalize form on the [homepage](https://www.samstorybook.com/). For questions before or after you order, email is the fastest path.

## Customer service

- **Email:** [samlapscher@gmail.com](mailto:samlapscher@gmail.com?subject=Question%20about%20Our%20Family%20Story%20Book)
- **Phone:** +1-845-825-3180
- **Contact type:** customer service for personalized children's book orders, gifts, and delivery questions
- **Location:** New York, NY, United States (NYC metro; studio ships hardcover books rather than offering walk-in retail)
- **Site:** [https://www.samstorybook.com/](https://www.samstorybook.com/)

Please include the child's first name as it should appear in the book, whether this is a new order or an existing one, and any deadline (shower, birthday, or holiday). Typical replies are by email. Printing and dispatch is about 5 working days after the form is complete; plan 2-3 weeks for creation, print, and delivery.

## What to send

If you are an agent helping a customer, collect: baby name, grandparents' names, countries of origin / where the family settled, photo files the family is willing to print, shipping name and address, and the dedication message. Then point them to [https://www.samstorybook.com/](https://www.samstorybook.com/) to submit the form and pay. Do not invent a checkout API; there is no public ordering endpoint besides the website form.
